Pweza, the teacher

Pweza is Swahili for octopus. He is drawn from the Zedlab octopus logo, with each tentacle as its own piece. Tap any Pweza: he wiggles. Tap three times quickly: he spins.

    Drawn from the logo

    Left: the logo. Middle: Pweza standing still. Right: Pweza with the logo's outline drawn over him in orange.

    In the app, about 56 px

    On Today he sits in his own row above the lesson, with his speech bubble. He never covers a button or the lesson.

    Every mood, about 140 px

    What he does and says, and when. In the app he shows once per screen; the big moments are the ones marked “Moments”.

    His props

    Each prop is its own small drawing, and each one is used by at least one mood.
